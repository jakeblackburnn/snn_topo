## Threads
### 1. Research question
Chosen (Jake): reopens `poster-cuts.md` thread 1. The bubble asks: "Can topological data analysis (TDA) extract meaningful signatures from recorded brain activity? Do those signatures tell real brain activity apart from spiking models?" This is Jake's pair with "topological" dropped before "signatures", since no topological difference is detected (`results.md` section 6); TDA still names the method. It carries a short answer, about 45 words: yes, with a catch. The signatures track engagement (11 of 12 sessions) and separate brains from models almost perfectly, but on the path's smoothness and noise, not its holes. The results follow the question, engagement first and then brain against model. This reverses the cuts spine, which put engagement last when it answered no question of its own. The cuts workshop had set aside the two-part question because its second half wasn't solid; section 6 has since answered it. Set aside: Jake's wording verbatim ("topological signatures" overclaims), the mirror question, and a bubble with the question only.
### 2. What it means and Next
Chosen (Jake): a new section V, Conclusions, after Limitations, about 70 words. It has two "so what" sentences that don't repeat the bubble answer (task-trained models lack the brain's trial-to-trial noise and dimensions; the signatures pick up behavioural state), then Next as 2-3 bullets: measure the models at the brain's noise, the no-go lead (`results.md:100-101`, with its noise caveat), single-trial paths. This replaces panels 12 and 13 (110 words). Set aside: folding Next into Limitations (no closing takeaway) and keeping both panels unchanged (repeats the bubble).
